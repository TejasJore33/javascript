It's a fantastic goal to dedicate 30 days to JavaScript with daily questions! This will solidify your understanding and help you practice consistently.

Below is a 30-day plan, with 10 questions for each day, covering a wide range of JavaScript topics from basics to more advanced concepts. I've tried to structure it progressively, but feel free to jump around if a topic sparks your interest.

How to Use This Plan:

Solve Actively: Don't just read the answers. Try to solve each question yourself, writing code and testing it.
Understand Concepts: Focus on understanding why a certain solution works, not just that it works.
Use Your Dev Tools: The browser's console (console.log()) is your best friend for testing small snippets. Node.js is great for backend-style practice.
Don't Get Stuck: If you're truly stuck on a question for more than 15-30 minutes, look up hints or even solutions, but then make sure you understand them thoroughly before moving on.
Review: Periodically review questions from previous days.
Expand: If a question sparks your interest, delve deeper into that topic.
